Who We Support

Supporting Children and Young People Up to Age 18

Nurture Pathways Care Group provides residential care and support for children and young people up to the age of 18.

Every Young Person Is Unique

Our approach is centred around individual needs, personality, interests, strengths and goals. We aim to provide a stable and welcoming home where young people can feel safe, develop confidence, build positive relationships and work towards greater independence.

We work collaboratively with families, professionals and relevant authorities to understand each young person's circumstances and determine whether our home and support can meet their individual needs.
