Our Care Services

Person-centred care, support and positive pathways

We provide a range of care and support approaches designed around individual needs, dignity, safety, independence and meaningful outcomes.

Six Areas of Support

Care designed around the individual

Our services are shaped around the person rather than a one-size-fits-all model. We aim to create supportive environments where people can feel respected, listened to and encouraged to achieve their personal goals.

01

Adult Mental Health

We understand that adults experiencing mental health challenges may benefit from a stable, respectful and structured environment. Our approach focuses on building trust, encouraging communication and supporting people to maintain meaningful routines and relationships.

Support can be built around an individual's existing care plan and identified needs. We encourage people to participate in decisions about their support, recognise their strengths and work towards realistic personal goals.

Our approach can include

  • Person-centred emotional wellbeing support.
  • Consistent routines and meaningful daily activities.
  • Encouragement to develop confidence and independence.
  • Support with communication, relationships and social participation.
  • Appropriate collaboration with families and relevant professionals.
  • Support focused on agreed outcomes and individual aspirations.

02

Neurological, Respiratory & Ventilation

People living with neurological or respiratory conditions can require carefully coordinated support that recognises their physical needs, routines, communication preferences and personal goals.

Our approach is centred on dignity, continuity and safe support. Where specialist or clinical requirements apply, support arrangements should be based on individual assessment, an agreed care plan and the involvement of appropriately qualified healthcare professionals.

Our approach can include

  • Individualised day-to-day support.
  • Support coordinated around agreed care plans.
  • Appropriate communication with healthcare professionals and support networks.
  • Promotion of dignity, comfort, choice and independence.
  • Support with routines and meaningful activities.
  • Care delivered within agreed competencies and service arrangements.

03

Learning Disabilities & Autism

We recognise that people with learning disabilities and autistic people have different strengths, communication styles, preferences and support needs. Our care approach is designed to respect those differences.

We aim to encourage choice, participation and independence while providing an environment where individuals can feel understood and supported. Everyday support can be used as an opportunity to develop confidence and practical skills.

Our approach can include

  • Individualised communication and support.
  • Support with everyday living and practical skills.
  • Encouraging choice, participation and independence.
  • Meaningful activities based around individual interests.
  • Support for appropriate community participation.
  • Respect for personal routines, preferences and ways of communicating.

04

Supported Living, Children & Young People

Our supported living and children's and young people's approach focuses on safety, stability, positive relationships and the development of confidence and independence.

For children and young people, Nurture Pathways provides residential care and support for children and young people up to the age of 18. Our current home is a three-bedroom setting with an activity room and sensory room, designed to provide a welcoming environment for development and everyday life.

We aim to support young people to develop practical life skills, take part in meaningful activities, build positive relationships and work towards their individual goals.

Our approach can include

  • Safe, warm and nurturing residential support.
  • Development of life skills and independence.
  • Support with routines, interests and meaningful activities.
  • Encouragement around education and positive future pathways.
  • Appropriate communication with families and professionals.
  • Individualised support based on assessed needs and placement suitability.

05

Active Assistance

Active assistance means supporting people to participate in their own lives rather than automatically doing things for them. We encourage individuals to take part, make choices and use their abilities wherever possible.

This approach can turn everyday activities into opportunities for confidence-building and skill development. The level of assistance is adapted to the individual's abilities, preferences and agreed support plan.

Our approach can include

  • Encouraging participation and personal choice.
  • Support with everyday household and personal routines.
  • Breaking activities into achievable steps.
  • Building confidence through positive reinforcement.
  • Encouraging hobbies, interests and community participation.
  • Developing practical skills for greater independence.

06

Case Management

Effective care often involves several people and services working together. Our case management approach focuses on clear communication, coordination and keeping the individual's goals at the centre of the support journey.

We aim to work collaboratively with individuals, families and relevant professionals so that support arrangements remain clear, appropriate and focused on agreed outcomes. Where needs change, appropriate reviews and communication can help ensure that support continues to reflect the person's circumstances.

Our approach can include

  • Coordinating appropriate support arrangements.
  • Communication between relevant people and professionals.
  • Goal-focused planning and outcome monitoring.
  • Reviewing changing needs and priorities.
  • Supporting continuity and clear information sharing.
  • Keeping the individual's preferences, dignity and goals central.
